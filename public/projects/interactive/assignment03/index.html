Skyhunt

Instructions

  • Click to shoot. 1 handgun, 2 shotgun, 3 sniper. R to reload.
  • Handgun: 2 dmg, 3 shots, medium radius, fast reload.
  • Shotgun: 1 dmg, 2 shots, very large radius, medium slow reload.
  • Sniper: 3 dmg, 1 shot, very precise radius, slow reload.
  • Everything inside the crosshair circle is hit.
  • Enemies fly up from the green strip. Some are faster or tankier.
  • Every 12 kills a 7 HP mini boss appears.
  • If 5 enemies escape you lose. Score and high score are shown on screen.
